About Sage

Singer-Songwriter · Author · Bend, Oregon

Hi, I'm Sage

I'm a 14-year-old singer-songwriter and fantasy author, now living permanently in Bend, Oregon.

I was born in Southern California and grew up between California, Abu Dhabi, Oregon, and Cambridge. For three years, my family and I lived aboard a catamaran and sailed through the Mediterranean and Caribbean. Now we're home in Bend, where I write fantasy novels, make music, bake, and spend time with friends.

I love writing and performing original music, free diving, baking (especially cookies and beef and bean chili), and hanging out with my friends.

The Music

I wrote my first song at eight years old, but it wasn't until I discovered Taylor Swift at eleven that writing really clicked for me. From there I was hooked — and I haven't stopped since.

I write and perform original songs, mostly inspired by real things that happen to me or the people around me. I play acoustic guitar and piano, and I work closely with my songwriting teacher Paulina Jayne.

You can find all my music on Instagram at @songs.by.sage. I post original songs and love connecting with people who listen.

The Books

I've always loved fantasy — magic, adventure, battles, and worlds where anything is possible. I wrote my first book at eleven and spent two years editing it before publishing it at thirteen.

That first book, Guardians of the Secret Isles: The Emerald Jaguar, is a middle grade fantasy adventure published in June 2025. It's the beginning of a five-book series, and there are more stories already in progress.

Writing taught me that creativity is powerful, but persistence is what turns imagination into reality. I'm proof that you don't have to wait to grow up to tell your story.

Three Years Under Sail

For three years, my family and I lived aboard a sailboat and explored the Mediterranean and Caribbean. We anchored in new places, met cruising families from around the world, and experienced things most people only read about.

I did Aerial Silks from the mast, free dived in clear water, watched sunsets from the cockpit with my guitar, and wrote wherever we were.

That chapter gave me endless material. Every island became a story, and every anchorage a new memory. Sailing is an important part of my story, even though Bend is home now.
